Privacy

Datenschutzerklärung for this site

This site has no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no forms. It loads nothing from other servers: fonts, images and styles all come from this site itself.

Who is responsible

This is a personal, non-commercial website. It is run by me, Kassem, reachable at contact@enkitech.de.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open a page, your browser sends GitHub your IP address and ordinary request details, as it would to any web server. GitHub may log these to keep the service running and secure; I don't receive or see those logs. This is in my legitimate interest in publishing a website reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details are in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Light or dark mode

If you click the light/dark switch, your choice is stored in your own browser (local storage, key enkitech_theme) so the next page opens the same way. It never leaves your device, and you can delete it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Links to other sites

Links to project sites, GitHub and LinkedIn take you to sites with their own privacy policies. Nothing is sent to them until you click. Sabura's policy is on the Sabura site.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of data about you, restrict or object to its processing, and complain to a data protection supervisory authority. Since this site itself collects nothing, the hosting logs above are held by GitHub; write to me and I will help where I can.
